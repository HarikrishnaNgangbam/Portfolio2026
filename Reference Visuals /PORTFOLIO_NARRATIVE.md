## Closing

> **Have a complex problem worth exploring?**

Communicate interest in **Design Leadership opportunities**, especially complex problems with meaningful stakes and room to rethink how things work.

## Case Study Philosophy

Case studies should answer:
- What was complex?
- Why was the existing model insufficient?
- What did Harikrishna believe?
- What system/principle did he design?
- How did it become an experience?
- What evidence supports the decisions?
- What changed?
- What did the work enable?

Show judgement, not just activity.

## Evidence

Use product visuals, videos, diagrams, metrics, before/after models, decision frameworks, flows, external coverage, deep links, screenshots, and outcomes.

Evidence should answer:
> **Why should I believe this?**

Never invent metrics, facts, or unsupported claims.

## Tone

Human, confident but not self-congratulatory, specific, thoughtful, senior, curious, direct.

Avoid generic design jargon, empty leadership claims, excessive corporate language, AI-sounding filler, promotional language, and unsupported claims.

## Narrative Decision Rule

> Prefer the option that reveals **how the designer thinks and makes decisions**, not merely what the designer delivered.
